    Limits we state openly

    All demand, supply and forecasts here are synthetic until NCS and scheme data are connected. PLFS 2025 is real but covers 12 months, and only 30 district × training-field cells (4 fields) reach 30 persons in Maharashtra, so district training capacity must come from scheme data. Weights are learned on synthetic data, so they show the method, not a finding. Ranges use correlations between series estimated from about two years of monthly errors, shrunk toward zero, so they are approximate.

    Extras beyond the brief

    Likelihood words on every call, a lever that says what to do, real course (QP) status, evidence level on every call, a planner decision log, seat-neutral rebalancing, a lead-time backtest, and refusal to call when evidence is thin.
