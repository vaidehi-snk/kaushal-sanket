Limits we state openly

All demand, supply and forecasts here are synthetic until NCS and scheme data are connected. PLFS 2025 is real but covers 12 months, and only 30 district × training-field cells (4 fields) reach 30 persons in Maharashtra, so district training capacity must come from scheme data. Weights are learned on synthetic data, so they show the method, not a finding. Ranges use correlations between series estimated from about two years of monthly errors, shrunk toward zero, so they are approximate. The occupation coder is a baseline with no accuracy claim yet.

Extras beyond the brief

Evidence level on every call, a planner decision log (closed loop, stage 1), seat-neutral rebalancing, a lead-time backtest, and refusal to call when evidence is thin.
